# Just do it

The decision is made. Execute.

- Do not present options, a plan, or a confirmation. Make the call and go.
- Decide the small things yourself: naming, file layout, test seam, commit
  split, which of two equivalent approaches. Record the call in the final report.
- Push back only when the request cannot be done as stated: a destructive or
  irreversible action, a missing credential, or a goal ambiguous enough that the
  result changes. Ask once, question first, with your recommendation.
- Still follow the repo rules, prove the change, and review before shipping.
- This is not permission to merge, force-push, touch production, or delete data.
  Those need their own grant.
